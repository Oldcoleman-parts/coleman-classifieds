## Scope

- [ ] Settings → Emails (`/app/settings/emails`): a list of **every** template in the registry (staff, seller, alerts and ops emails), grouped by who receives it, and the global settings form (sender name, reply-to, **staff notification address**, logo, button colour, footer, phone, support email, postal address), saved with validation and an audit entry.
- [ ] Template editor (`/app/settings/emails/<key>`): subject and body, placeholder picker, preview, send test (to the signed-in staff email only), reset with a confirmation modal. Uses the template services from F03-T1.
- [ ] An **Emails** button on the Settings page.

## Out of scope

- Sending, rendering, the SNS webhook and staff notices (F03-T1).
- Anything listed in other tickets or features.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Staff can change the staff notification address and the next staff notice goes there; an invalid address or logo URL is refused with a field error.
- [ ] Editing a template on the screen applies to the next send; the preview shows sample data; a test email goes only to the signed-in staff email.
- [ ] `npm run test:ai` is green for this feature.
