## Scope

- [x] SNS webhook (`/webhooks/ses`): signature verification, subscription confirm, hard bounce and complaint → suppression; EMAIL incident on failures or high rates.

## Notes

**Split:** the Settings → Emails screens and the global email settings form moved to F03-T3. This ticket is the engine; its template services (`saveTemplate`, `resetTemplate`, `previewTemplate`) are what those screens will call.

**Schema:** migration `20261005103115_add_email_outbox` adds `EmailOutbox` (the send queue). No template-history table: `EmailTemplate.version` increments and the audit log records each edit (`EmailTemplateUpdated`, `EmailTemplateReset`, with the key and version, not the wording).

**Outside this feature's modules (approved when the ticket was planned):**

- `core.getListingSummary(id)` (`app/modules/core/listings.js`): a read-only `{ id, title, status }` so staff notices can show the title without querying core's table.
- `app/bootstrap.server.js`: registers the notifications module.

**Decisions**

- Queue: every email gets an outbox row, is tried at once, and on a temporary SES error is retried by the hourly task `notifications.retry-outbox` (waits of 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h, 24 h; six tries, then `DEAD`). Errors that won't change on a retry go straight to `FAILED`. Finished rows are deleted after 30 days and their data is cleared as soon as they are sent.
- Emails that carry a code or private link (`sensitive` in `registry.js`: reminders and both alert emails) never have their data stored. They are tried up to three times on the spot and the caller gets `FAILED` if that doesn't work.
- Logs hold the outbox id, template key and error code only.
- Until a stream's sender and configuration set are in the environment, its emails wait in the queue rather than fail.
- SNS verification is hand-written with `node:crypto` (no extra dependency). The endpoint answers **403** to anything it can't verify, as the acceptance criterion says (the add-webhook skill says 401). Repeated deliveries need no receipt table: suppressing an address twice changes nothing.
- Email problems: `ops` (F08) owns incidents. Until then `reportEmailProblem()` writes an `EmailProblem` audit entry and a log line, at most once a day per problem. Thresholds: bounces above 5% or complaints above 0.1% of a day's sends, once at least 20 were sent.
- Templates: Liquid with plain `{{ placeholder }}` outputs, `if`/`unless`, and `for` only over a template's own lists; no filters, no other tags. The body is sanitised when saved and again on every rendered email.
- Every alerts-stream email needs `unsubscribe_link`, the confirmation email too; the layout adds the link and postal address, so staff can't remove them. Alert emails aren't sent while the postal address is empty.
- Links passed to a template must be `https`.
- The sender address comes from `SES_FROM_*` per stream; `EmailSettings.fromAddress` is seeded from it.
- Staff notices carry the title, the change type and the link; held-message notices carry reason codes only. Each notice is keyed on the event's audit id, so a repeated event sends nothing new.
- Review links point at `/app/listings/<id>` and `/app/messages` inside Shopify admin (new env var `SHOP_DOMAIN`). Those screens arrive with F05, so the links 404 until then.
- `invitation` is seeded as wording only; `send()` refuses it.
- At the user's request every staff and seller email has an editable template in this feature. The ten seller templates were added with drafted wording (the spec had none): `email_code`, `changes_requested`, `listing_rejected`, `approved_pay`, `listing_live`, `expiry_reminder`, `listing_closed` (one template, wording chosen by `close_reason`), `revision_approved`, `revision_rejected`, `buyer_message`. Phase 2 renewal emails are left to F09 so they stay hidden until released.
- Default bodies for the five staff and ops templates were drafted here and added to `docs/reference/email-default-content.md`.
- New packages: `@aws-sdk/client-sesv2`, `liquidjs`, `sanitize-html`, `html-to-text`.
