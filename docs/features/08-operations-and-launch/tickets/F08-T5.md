## Scope

- [ ] `platform`: `onPrivacyRequest(handler)`, exported from `app/modules/platform/index.js` and called by `handlePrivacyWebhook` for `CUSTOMERS_REDACT` and `SHOP_REDACT`. This is a cross-module edit and needs approval.
- [ ] `app/modules/ops/services/erase.js`: `eraseCustomer({ email })` and `eraseShop()`, both idempotent. They change these rows:
  - `Invitation`: the seller name becomes `Redacted`, the email becomes `redacted+<id>@invalid`, the phone becomes null;
  - `BuyerMessage`: name, email, body and ip are blanked;
  - `AlertSubscriber` rows for the email are deleted;
  - `EmailOutbox` rows to that address are deleted;
  - `EmailSuppression` for the email is deleted;
  - `TermsAcceptance`: ip and userAgent are blanked.
- [ ] Kept as they are: `FeeCharge` is kept, photos follow normal retention, and audit rows stay until retention.
