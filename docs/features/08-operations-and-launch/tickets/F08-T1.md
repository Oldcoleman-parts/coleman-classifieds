## Scope

- [ ] Lifecycle tasks: month expiry and reminders; invitation, draft (+ reminder), correction and payment deadlines; payment reminder and lapse; reconcile missed `orders/paid`; `live_count` drift check.
- [ ] Retention: delete private photos after 90 days; purge or anonymise records past `purgeAfter` with the purge role; delete old metaobjects + create redirects.
- [ ] Phase 2 and Later task hooks (renewals, unverified submissions) that return immediately while their flags are off.

## Out of scope

- Anything listed in other tickets or features.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every task is idempotent (two runs → one effect), and deadlines close listings on the next run.
- [ ] Records older than 1 year after close are gone; the AuditEvent purge only works through the purge role.
- [ ] One failing task doesn’t stop the others.
- [ ] `npm run test:ai` is green for this feature.
